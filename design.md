# Forewise locked design system

Owner-approved direction: Forecast Horizon for public cinematic storytelling; Fieldnotes for operational screens. This is an existing product, not a theme-dealing exercise. Hallmark is authorized for audit and polish on 25 October 2026. No full redesign has been requested.

- Preserve the real Forewise wordmark, DM Sans Variable body and existing Georgia serif accents. Fonts are self-hosted through `@fontsource-variable/dm-sans`; no new type download or replacement.
- Operational palette: paper `#f3f0e8`, surface `#fffdf8`, ink `#282a25`, muted `#66695f`, accent `#ad4228`, accent surface `#f4e4da`. Read/update the shared role tokens in `apps/web/app/globals.css`; preserve the palette while improving contrast and legibility where measured.
- Public Horizon palette: background `#080f11`, text `#e7efeb`, mint `#a8efcf`, muted `#b6c8c0`. Variants Observatory and Atlas remain separate existing explorations. Read `apps/web/app/horizon.css` and `docs/design/horizon/REVIEW.md` for their actual implementation.
- Preserve the optional Three.js experience, native 2D/reduced-motion fallback, controls, keyboard/table alternatives, renderer lifecycle/performance and explicitly synthetic spatial storytelling. Native model forecasts belong in the operational dashboard, not in decorative 3D probability claims.
- Preserve responsive layout and working routes, CTAs, forms, session/tenant boundaries, async worker behavior, IDs and test hooks. A visual polish must not change model contracts or calibrate intervals by appearance.
- The existing small section labels, observation metadata and navigation iconography are part of the approved identity. Evaluate their readability and redundancy in context; do not strip them merely to obtain a third-party style score. Mathematical ranges such as P10–P90 and missing-value marks retain their meaning.
- Hallmark contrast, focus, honest content, reduced-motion and persistent-overflow findings require actual checks and correction. Hallmark house-style conflicts and parser false positives must be explicitly reported and reviewed against this lock; never claim they are raw passes or silently waive them.
- No fabricated numbers, testimonials, customers, pricing, photos or backend functionality. No paid Together AI/image-generation service, global hook or deployment is implied by adopting Hallmark.
