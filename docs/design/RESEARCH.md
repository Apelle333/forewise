# Research and design-engineering references

24 October 2026. Sources below were opened live; observations are design interpretation, not claims of copying their implementation. Browser captures of Linear, Stripe, Ramp, Vercel and Recent are in `references/` with response metadata. Other references were examined through their primary pages/docs. Godly redirected to Recent; Bloomberg and NYT pages were inaccessible and are not counted as reviewed references. Early Linear/Recent frames were incomplete during load; refreshed captures are retained where available.

## Technology decision

Keep the current runtime dependencies and original CSS/SVG components. Add only `@axe-core/playwright` 4.14.0 as a development dependency (MPL-2.0 verified via npm metadata) for automated accessibility checks; nothing is bundled into the product. Existing fonts remain locally bundled. No purchased asset, new hosted service, external AI call or transferred customer data is needed.
