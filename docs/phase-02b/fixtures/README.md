# Evaluation fixture provenance

`air-passengers.csv`: 144 real monthly international airline passenger totals, January 1949–December 1960, in thousands. Public historical AirPassengers series from the R datasets package, attributed there to Box & Jenkins (1976), *Time Series Analysis: Forecasting and Control*.

- [R primary documentation](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/datasets/html/AirPassengers.html)
- [Rdatasets CSV mirror](https://vincentarelbundock.github.io/Rdatasets/csv/datasets/AirPassengers.csv)

Converted the mirror's time index to ISO month-start dates and renamed the measurement to `value`; no imputation, synthetic rows, rescaling or target modifications. R documentation/package distribution retains its own notices; this file is numerical historical data, not copied explanatory prose. No ownership or business-validation claim. Pretraining contamination is unknown.

The other five benchmark fixtures are explicitly synthetic and generated by `scripts/benchmark-timestral.py`, seed 20261025: daily demand, weekly sales, monthly revenue, seasonal and intermittent demand. See the exact generator and cutoffs in the benchmark report. They are reproducibility probes, not customer results.
